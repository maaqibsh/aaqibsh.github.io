Sheikh Muhammad Aaqib

Power Electronics Research · The University of Tennessee, Knoxville

I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, working with Prof. Jiangbiao He. I design wide-bandgap (SiC and GaN) power converters for electric vehicles, energy storage and DC microgrids, and I work across the whole hardware cycle: topology and component sizing, control-board and gate-driver PCB design, DSP firmware, electrothermal modeling, and high-power testing.

My current work is a DOE-funded 150 kW integrated charger-inverter system that lets one SiC power stage drive the traction motor and also charge the vehicle from AC or DC sources. Before this I built a 15 kW GaN quad-active-bridge converter that links PV, battery, EV and DC-grid ports through a single high-frequency transformer.

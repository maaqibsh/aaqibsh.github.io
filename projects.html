Ph.D. · current · 2026 – present

150 kW SiC integrated EV charger-inverter

A single SiC power stage (DAB plus three-phase inverter with LCL filter) that works as the traction motor drive and as an AC or DC charger. I designed the DSP control board, sized the power stage and am writing the DAB modulation firmware. Now in testing, with a dynamometer setup being built.

  • SiC
  • DAB
  • Three-phase inverter
  • LCL filter
  • PCB design
  • DSP firmware

Ph.D. · completed · 2025 – 2026

15 kW GaN quad-active-bridge converter

A four-port isolated DC-DC converter linking PV, battery, EV and the DC grid. I designed the converter, PCB layout and gate drivers, wrote the power-flow control firmware with SPS/EPS/DPS modulation, did all of the testing, and modeled transformer losses thermally.

  • GaN
  • QAB
  • Phase-shift modulation
  • Soft switching
  • Thermal modeling
